Report infringing content

If you are a rights holder or authorized agent and believe content on clever-teaching.com infringes your copyright, send a written notice to our designated agent:

Designated Copyright Agent

Name: Clever-Teaching

Email: dmca@clever-teaching.com

Your notice must include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature, or that of a person authorized to act for the rights holder.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed.
  3. Identification of the material you claim is infringing, with its exact URL(s) or file name(s), detailed enough for us to locate it.
  4. Your contact details: name, address, telephone number and email.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on the owner's behalf.

What we do when we receive a notice

  • We review complete notices promptly.
  • If a notice is valid, we remove or disable access to the identified material, and we confirm the action to the sender.
  • We notify the affected party, where one exists, and give them the chance to submit a counter-notice.
  • Incomplete notices may be returned with a request for the missing information.

Counter-notice

If you believe material was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notice to the agent above that includes your signature, identification of the removed material and where it appeared, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith the removal was a mistake, your name, address and phone number, and consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your district. Knowingly false notices or counter-notices can carry legal liability.

Repeat infringers

We terminate access for users or contributors who are repeatedly found to have infringed copyright.
